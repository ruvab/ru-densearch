Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Your step-by-step execution plan for landing a Danish FinTech role.

Phase 1: Preparation (One-Time)

  • Format CV to Danish standards: Remove photo, age, and marital status. Focus heavily on technical competencies.
  • Highlight Agile certifications (PSM, PMP) and Core Banking/Payment Gateway expertise prominently on the first page.
  • Update your LinkedIn profile location to "Copenhagen Metropolitan Area" (Crucial for recruiter searches).

Phase 2: Daily Execution

  • Morning (15 mins): Scan Workindenmark.dk, TheHub.io, and LinkedIn for new English-speaking postings in FinTech.
  • Afternoon (30-60 mins): Submit tailored applications. In Cover Letters, emphasize "cross-functional collaboration in a flat hierarchy" over direct people management.
  • Tracking: Log every application immediately into the tab.

Phase 3: Weekly Networking

  • Identify 2-3 internal recruiters or Talent Acquisition Managers at target companies (e.g., Danske Bank, Nets) on LinkedIn.
  • Send targeted connection requests: "Hi [Name], I'm a Senior BA specializing in Digital Payments/Core Banking looking to relocate to Copenhagen. I admire [Company]'s recent work in [Project] and would love to connect."
  • Review your tracker every Friday to follow up on any applications older than 14 days.
